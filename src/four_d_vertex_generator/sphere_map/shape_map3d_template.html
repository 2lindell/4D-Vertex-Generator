Swirlprism shape map

Each named shape of the h4 swirlprism symmetry with everything that touches it, placed where it lies on the 3-sphere (projected from the point opposite the selected shape). Each node's glyph has the rotational symmetry of its piece on the sphere: one petal for a piece with no symmetry, five for a piece that five copies of the group leave in place. Pieces of one wall or line that touch are one node. Drag to turn, scroll to zoom, select a node or a chip to move to it.
