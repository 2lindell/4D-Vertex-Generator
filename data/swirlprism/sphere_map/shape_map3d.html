Swirlprism shape map

Each named shape of the h4 swirlprism symmetry with everything that touches it, placed where it lies on the 3-sphere (projected from the point opposite the selected shape). A wall is a tile in its own plane, a line a rod along it, a region a box turned to its own shape (a cube, or a thin slab for a thin region), a point a small solid; walls, lines and points carry the symmetry of the shape on the sphere (a cut corner or an arrowhead for none, n fins or n sides for n-fold). A node is a whole named shape: a wall up to the named lines that bound it, a line from named point to named point. Drag to turn, scroll to zoom, select a node or a chip to move to it.
