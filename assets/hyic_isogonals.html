Hyic Isogonals

There are 65 isogonal polychora with one or more of the hyic family symmetries (H4 = [5,3,3] and the groups around it) listed below, by the symmetry under which each is isogonal and no larger one. Ixoic symmetry is still being mapped.

Every entry is written from the polychoron itself at the seed given (β = distances to the four H4 mirrors; for ixitic, on the sheet named): cells by orbit, vertices with the dualcell's face count, edges by how many faces meet there, faces by shape. Names and full names are from the accepted list; the 49 shapes marked new were found by the atlas and are not yet named.

Hyics

Hyic symmetry [5,3,3] order 14400. This is the symmetry of the 120-cell and is quite common in the uniform polychora. This symmetry has 120 points of doic symmetry, 600 points of tettic symmetry, 720 points of pippic symmetry, and 1200 points of trippic symmetry. It has 72 rings with decagonal symmetry that generate 1440 spots with pentagon symmetry, 200 rings with hexagonal symmetry that generate 2400 trigonic regions, and 450 rings with square symmetry that create 3600 rectic areas. There are also 60 spheres with doic symmetry that create 7200 dyadic regions. White space creates 14400 points with no symmetry. As a reflection group with no diagram symmetry, each face of its chamber is one shape: the fifteen Wythoffian polychora of the 120-cell family below.

Kihyics

It has 120 points of kidoic symmetry, 600 points of kitettic symmetry, 720 points of kipippic symmetry, and 1200 points of kitrippic symmetry. It has 72 rings with decagonal symmetry that create 1440 kipeggic regions, 200 hexagonal rings that generate 2400 kitriggic regions, and 450 square rings that create 3600 essic regions. White space creates 7200 spots with no symmetry. The map of its chamber is one region: snixhi.

Ixitics

Ixitic symmetry 5[3⁺,4,3⁺] order 1440. It has two sets of 120 points with kitettic symmetry. It has 80 rings with kihiggic symmetry which generate 480 kitriggic areas and 90 rings with square symmetry that generate 720 essic areas. White space generates 1440 no sym areas. It has index 10 in hyic, so its domain is ten copies of the hyic chamber (ten sheets), each mapped by curved walls; the mirrors are seams between sheets. Ixoics

Ixoic symmetry 5[[3⁺,4,3⁺]] order 2880. It has 240 points with kitettic symmetry, two sets of 480 points with kitrippic symmetry, and 720 points with kibrick symmetry. It has 80 hexagonal rings which generate 960 kitriggic areas and 90 octagonal rings that generate 1440 essic areas. White space creates 2880 no sym areas. It is not inside hyic (its elements need √2), so it has no exact wall forms over the golden field and each sheet is mapped numerically. The ten sheets are being mapped; this section is filled in when they are done.
