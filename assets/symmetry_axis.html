Around a 2400-symmetry axis

The shapes nearest one of the axes of the extra half-turns (the 2400-element group's elements outside the swirlprism group), each shown once and moved out from the axis so each stands apart. The axis is the purple line; the view is gnomonic, as in the domain page, centred on the axis.

drag to turn · scroll to zoom · hover a shape
  • Each shape is shown with only the extent its seeds need once: a shape the 2400-element group carries onto itself is cut to the part no symmetry repeats (X38, turned onto itself by the axis's half-turn: its half on one side of the axis); of a shape and its copies, one is shown (X68's two sides of X38, which the half-turn carries onto each other: one side).
  • Walls are solid; a region is shown by its shell, the walls bounding it, see-through.
