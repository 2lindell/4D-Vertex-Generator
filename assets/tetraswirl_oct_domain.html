Hover a point for its shape and seed; click it to pin those details here and copy the seed. Click a row in the key to show only that shape, Ctrl-click (⌘-click on a Mac) to add or remove more, and click the only selected row again to show everything.

Key

Counts are symmetry classes of cells, faces and edges: 8×[6v8f] is a class of 8 cells with 6 vertices and 8 faces, 16×3g a class of 16 triangles, 24×4 a class of 24 edges each on 4 cells. The example seed can be pasted into the generator's Coordinates mode.
