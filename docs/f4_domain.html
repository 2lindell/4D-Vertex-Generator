Symmetry

Key

Counts are symmetry classes of cells, faces and edges: 10×[24v14f] is a class of 10 cells with 24 vertices and 14 faces, 40×3g a class of 40 triangles, 120×3 a class of 120 edges each on 3 cells. The example seed can be pasted into the generator's Coordinates mode with the same symmetry; β are its distances to the four mirrors.
