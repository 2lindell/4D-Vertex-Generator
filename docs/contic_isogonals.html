Icoic and Contic Isogonals

There are 120 isogonal polychora with one or more of the icoic family symmetries (F4 = [3,4,3] and the groups around it) listed below, by the symmetry under which each is isogonal and no larger one.

Every entry is written from the polychoron itself at the seed given (β = distances to the four F4 mirrors, b1 and b2 long, b3 and b4 short): cells by orbit, vertices with the dualcell's face count, edges by how many faces meet there, faces by shape. Names and full names are from the accepted lists; the 41 shapes marked new were found by the atlas and are not yet named.
