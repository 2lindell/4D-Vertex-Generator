Swirlprism seed atlas

Every seed point for this symmetry is equivalent to one inside this dodecahedron: the points closer to the starting 600-cell vertex than to any other. Lines are the cross rings (600 vertices) and the main ring (240), marked points are the named special shapes, and the clouds are the 1200-vertex shapes found by sampling the space in between.

Hover or tap a point to see its seed. Click a row in the key below to show only that shape; click it again to show everything.

How to read the atlas

  • The view is a gnomonic projection centred on the starting vertex: great circles are straight lines, so every cross ring and main ring is a straight segment.
  • The vertical axis through the centre is the main ring. The five horizontal lines through the centre are the cross rings; the other lines are cross rings through other 600-cell vertices, passing through this region.
  • The 20 corners of the dodecahedron are 120-cell vertices. The centres of the top and bottom faces are vertices of the second 600-cell.
  • Only one tenth of the dodecahedron was sampled. The other nine tenths are copies under the symmetries that fix the starting vertex.
  • Grey points are samples right on a boundary between two shapes, where the hull is degenerate.
  • Dashed outlines (toggle H4 mirrors) are the 15 mirrors of full H4 that cross the region. All pass through the centre: 5 vertical ones containing the main ring, halfway between the cross rings, and 10 tilted ones.
  • Types marked surface only occur only on one of the special planes through the centre (the vertical planes through the main ring and a cross ring, and the horizontal cross-ring plane). They separate two volume types.

Using a point in the generator

  • Hover a point and copy its seed into the app's Coordinates seed mode with the small swirlprism subgroup selected.
  • A plot position (x, y, z) is the seed 1, 0.850651·y − 0.525731·z, x, −0.525731·y − 0.850651·z, normalised.
  • Sampling spacing is 0.02 in plot units (about 1.1° near the centre), so thin regions and single points can be missed. Named shapes that occur at single points are placed from the exact ring analysis, not from sampling.
