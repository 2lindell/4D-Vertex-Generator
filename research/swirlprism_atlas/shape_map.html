Shape map

Every shape in the atlas, coloured by dimension. Select one and it moves to the middle, with the shapes on its boundary and the shapes it bounds arranged around it, each placed in the direction it lies from the selected shape in the cell. Rings count down the dimensions from the selected shape outwards, wrapping from points round to regions (3, 2, 1, 0, 3, …).
