Where walls cross

The atlas maps took lines and points only on each chamber's own edges and corners. The crossing search follows every pair (and triple) of a map's walls where they cross inside the chamber and classifies the polychoron there. Each shape below kept the same polychoron at three hull tolerances, kept it moving along its locus out to 0.001, changed it 0.00001 off each of its walls, and has exactly its group's symmetry (no larger one). None is on the accepted lists.
