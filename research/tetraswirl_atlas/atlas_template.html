Method

Each coloured volume is a region of the group’s gnomonic fundamental domain with constant convex-hull combinatorics. Translucent surfaces mark the boundaries where five orbit points become coplanar; lines and points mark their intersections. The atlas is a numerical classification, with examples independently checked against the orbit hull.
